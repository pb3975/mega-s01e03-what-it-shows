Shared parser reads day-first dates month first

Parser ticket. The shared parser assumes month-first input although these tenants use day-first dates. For example, R-41200 at harbour.dental reports 7 April becoming 4 July. This affects app and email reports and forms whose hint is already day-first. Changing a form hint alone cannot fix those paths. Make the parser respect an explicit input contract and verify ambiguous dates on every affected path.

Booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to enter month first

Form hint ticket. For day-first tenants, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY while its form reads dates the tenant’s way. This is a different failure: a person can follow the displayed instructions and still get the wrong date. R-41241 at alder.physio reports 6 August becoming 8 June. It arrives through the form; the tenant runs booking-v3. Align the hint with the form’s interpretation and check it separately from the shared parser.

The one-day-move reports are retained in the parser group and footnoted by the component. Their bodies raise a follow-up question about timezones, date-only handling, and chronology. Tenant classification is not proof of each report's root cause. Do not silently subtract these reports or claim a confirmed third bug without reproduction evidence.

The follow-up report IDs are R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, and R-41620. R-41236 even describes an invite arriving before the booking. Preserve these accounts and ask for timestamps, input, expected date, actual date, and timezone.

Read arrival time separately from merge time

Reports arrived from April through October. The large July merge bar describes desk processing, not the start of a sudden product incident. Later arrivals keep being merged into the known ticket. A queued parser fix therefore cannot stand in for a ticket for the form-hint failure, and “known” does not mean resolved.

Proposed desk rule: read the body, resolve tenant locale and form, check the channel, and compare the failure before merging. Keep uncertain reports separate until that evidence exists. Link each confirmed cause to its own ticket and review the live component output before marking the page known.
